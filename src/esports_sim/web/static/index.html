How to play

You run the org. Each week:

Roster — watch form/morale/stamina, renew expiring contracts, hold your weekly 1:1 (Talk), hire backroom staff (coach, analyst, physio).
Dashboard — set the training focus (rest when the team is gassed).
Standings — click a rival to see their roster; assign your scout to sharpen their numbers over ~3 weeks.
Market — sign free agents (roster is 5; release first, severance applies).
Finances — sponsorship offers expire after one week; pick the shape that fits your season.
Advance Week ▸ — matches play out; hit ▶ on your result to watch the replay. Top 4 make the BO3 playoffs (map veto applies).

Same campaign seed + same decisions = the same season, every time.
