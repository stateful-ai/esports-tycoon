How to play

You run the org. Each week:

Club — your squad HQ: watch form/morale/confidence, set each player's development plan (focus + intensity), name your starting five, renew contracts, hold your weekly 1:1 (Talk), and run the academy, culture and match prep.
Dashboard — set the team training focus (rest when the team is gassed).
Facilities — build permanent club departments, compare current and next-level benefits, and manage their weekly upkeep.
Season — league tables, fixtures, playoffs and records; click any team name for their profile.
Market — sign free agents (5 start, bench up to 7), scout the field, and hire backroom staff (coach, analyst, physio) from a shared pool.
Finances — sponsorship offers expire after one week; pick the shape that fits your season.
Advance Week ▸ — matches play out; hit ▶ on your result to watch the replay. Top 4 make the BO3 playoffs (map veto applies).

The same setup and decisions will always produce the same season.
