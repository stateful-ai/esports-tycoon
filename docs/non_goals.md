# Non-goals

Things Esports Tycoon deliberately is *not*. Listed so they can be
declined quickly.

- **Not a real-time strategy game.** Matches resolve deterministically
  from a roster + tactical inputs, not via realtime control.
- **Not multiplayer.** Single-player tycoon experience; no leaderboards.
- **Not a content-generation showcase.** Templates carry the prose load;
  the LLM is opt-in for moments where templated text feels flat.
- **Not a sports simulator with broad league depth.** One fictional
  esport; broad sports support is out of scope.
- **Not metrics-driven.** No retention or DAU goals.
