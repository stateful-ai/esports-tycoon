## Doc: Esports Tycoon Scene Shaping

### Core loop
The core loop focuses on the player's ability to shape the atmosphere and dynamics of their esports scene through strategic decisions on event organization, fan engagement, and team culture. The loop is driven by the player's desire to create memorable moments and a unique identity for their scene, which in turn influences fan loyalty and team performance.

### Vertical slice
A vertical slice for this core loop involves organizing a special event. The player decides on the theme, selects participating teams, and chooses activities that will engage fans and players alike. The player then observes the outcomes, such as fan attendance, team morale, and media coverage, which reflect the success of their decisions. This slice can be completed in under two hours, fitting into a weekend test.

### Mechanics
- **Event Planning Interface**: The player accesses a menu to choose an event theme (e.g., Retro Night, International Showdown) from a list of available options, each with a resource cost and thematic restrictions (e.g., Retro Night requires older equipment). The player selects participating teams from a pool of available teams, each with a morale threshold and a cost based on their current performance and availability. Activities are chosen from a list, each with a specific impact on fan engagement and team interaction (e.g., live music increases fan attendance by 10%, interactive challenges boost team morale by 5%).
- **Decision Points**:
  - Theme selection affects fan interest by a fixed percentage (e.g., Retro Night increases fan interest by 20%) and media attention by a fixed score (e.g., Retro Night attracts a media score of 3 out of 5).
  - Team selection impacts team morale by a variable percentage based on the team's current morale and the event's theme compatibility (e.g., a high-morale team at a Retro Night event might see a 15% boost in morale).
  - Activity choices influence fan engagement by a fixed percentage (e.g., live music increases fan engagement by 10%) and team interaction by a variable score based on the activity's nature and the team's personality traits (e.g., a team with a high 'competitive' trait might see a 7-point increase in team interaction from interactive challenges).
- **Execution Phase**: Once the event is set, the player watches a short animated sequence showcasing the event, highlighting key moments like fan reactions, team performances, and media coverage. The sequence dynamically adjusts based on the player’s decisions, showing more enthusiastic fans if fan engagement is high, and more cohesive team interactions if team interaction scores are high.
- **Outcome Feedback**: After the event, the player receives detailed feedback on fan attendance (e.g., increased by 20%), team morale boosts (e.g., improved by 15%), and media coverage (e.g., media score increased by 3 points). These impacts directly influence future fan loyalty and team performance metrics, such as increasing fan loyalty by 10% for every 10% increase in fan attendance and boosting team performance by 5% for every 10% increase in team morale.
