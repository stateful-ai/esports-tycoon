## Doc: Esports Tycoon Roster Trade Impact Visualization

### Goal
Implement a visual and dynamic representation of how trades affect the team's overall performance and synergy to make players feel like they are shaping the scene.

### Tickets (sequenced)
1. **Ticket 1: Develop Synergy Indicator Visuals**
   - **Concern**: Create visual indicators that show the synergy between players on the team.
   - **Acceptance Test**: When viewing a trade offer, players can see a visual representation of how the new player would fit into the team's existing dynamics, including color-coded compatibility scores.
   - **Non-goal**: Integration with other game mechanics beyond trade decisions.

2. **Ticket 2: Implement Trade Offer Notification**
   - **Concern**: Ensure players are notified of trade offers in a way that feels natural to the game world.
   - **Acceptance Test**: Players receive a notification with a visual cue and sound when a trade offer is made, including basic information about the offer.
   - **Non-goal**: Detailed evaluation metrics for the trade offer.

3. **Ticket 3: Integrate Immediate Impact Feedback**
   - **Concern**: Provide immediate feedback on how a trade affects the team's stats and synergy.
   - **Acceptance Test**: After accepting or declining a trade, players see an update on the team's stats and a visual change in the synergy indicators, with a summary of the impact shown.
   - **Non-goal**: Long-term effects of trades on the game world outside of the immediate team.

### Deferred / non-goals
- Automated trade suggestions based on AI analysis (deferred due to high development cost and complexity).
- Historical impact tracking of past trades on team performance (deferred due to limited resources and focus on immediate feedback).
- Integration with external league standings or rival team dynamics (deferred to avoid scope creep and maintain focus on core gameplay).
