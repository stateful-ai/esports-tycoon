Inbox

Work through decisions and deadlines without losing them in league news.

START HERE
Handle Actionable Items first; League Feed is context, not a to-do list.
WATCH FOR
Buttons are based on the current campaign state, so an old message cannot trigger an expired offer.
Open Inbox
Match

Prepare the dressed five, tactics, scouting plan, and fixture-specific game plan.

START HERE
Check the opponent report, then adjust only the dials or lineup you have a reason to change.
WATCH FOR
Extreme tactics create real trade-offs. Neutral settings are safe while you learn the roster.
Open Match
Club

Manage the squad, weekly development, locker room, academy, and staff delegation.

START HERE
Name a default five, check condition and confidence, then set team and player training plans.
WATCH FOR
Bench players still develop, but slower, and established players eventually expect minutes.
Open Club
Facilities

Invest in permanent departments that support training, preparation, recovery, and wellbeing.

START HERE
Compare the next-level benefit with both the build cost and weekly upkeep.
WATCH FOR
Upgrades are long-term commitments; do not spend the wage budget needed to keep the roster legal.
Open Facilities
Season

Track the table, schedule, playoff cut, records, and the rest of the league.

START HERE
Use Fixtures for timing and League for the playoff race; team names open full profiles.
WATCH FOR
Round difference and head-to-head results can matter when records are tied.
Open Season
Market

Find players and staff who improve the club without breaking its finances or chemistry.

START HERE
Scout before paying for certainty, and compare role, languages, ceiling, salary, and contract cost together.
WATCH FOR
A higher overall is not automatically a better fit for your lineup, system, or budget.
Open Market
Stats

Separate repeatable performance from short streaks and identify league-wide strengths.

START HERE
Begin with Leaders, then use team and map splits to test the story behind the headline numbers.
WATCH FOR
Your analytics department controls how much detail is available; locked columns are a staff/facility problem.
Open Stats
Company

Keep the club solvent while growing sponsors, supporters, reach, and public sentiment.

START HERE
Check weekly net cash flow before accepting costs, then review sponsor demands and brand momentum.
WATCH FOR
Results, player reach, and public decisions affect the commercial side of the club over time.
Open Company
