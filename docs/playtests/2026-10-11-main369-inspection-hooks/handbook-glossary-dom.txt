KEY TERMS
OVR / current ability
What the player can deliver now. Role comfort, confidence, form, and tactics still shape match performance.
Ceiling / potential
A forecast of a player's likely peak, not a guaranteed maximum or hard cap.
Form
Recent performance. Useful, but noisier than attributes and a longer run of match data.
Confidence
Belief under pressure; it affects duels and clutch nerve and moves back toward neutral over time.
Condition
Physical readiness. Matches and hard training drain it; rest and recovery support restore it.
Fog / scouting range
Uncertainty in an external read. More scouting narrows the band but never reveals private state perfectly.
Role comfort
Familiarity with the current role and playstyle assignment. A reassignment starts low and settles with time.
Badge
A public career marker earned from match or development events. Hover it to see its exact effect and how it can fade.
xDuel / xDE
Actual duel wins compared with expected duel wins. It helps distinguish strong results from favorable matchups.
Hover for detail
Most numbers, abbreviations, badges, controls, and table headings have tooltips. Keyboard users can focus badge and info icons to read the same explanations.
