## Doc: Esports Tycoon Next Milestone Recommendation

### Recommendation
Implement a dynamic event system that introduces unexpected challenges and opportunities, such as sudden player injuries, rival team scandals, or fan riots, which require the player to adapt their strategy and management decisions in real-time. This will make players feel like they are shaping a living, breathing scene rather than managing a static spreadsheet.

### What we give up
By focusing on dynamic events, we delay the implementation of other features such as deeper financial management tools or more detailed player customization options. We would reconsider this call if player testing shows a preference for more predictable and controllable aspects of management over the excitement of handling unpredictable events.

### Signals
- **1-week checkpoint**: Player feedback indicates increased engagement and excitement when faced with dynamic events, as measured by survey responses and forum discussions.
- **1-month checkpoint**: Retention rates increase by at least 10% among players who have experienced the dynamic event system, indicating that the feature is successfully making players feel more involved in a dynamic scene.
